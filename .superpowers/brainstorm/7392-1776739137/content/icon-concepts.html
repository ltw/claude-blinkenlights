Icon concepts

Four directions. Each rendered as a macOS app icon (squircle). Click one to pick a direction — we'll refine from there.

A — HDD bezel

Classic beige 3.5" drive front, green activity LED on the right. Literal and nostalgic.

B — Blinkenlights row

Literal "blinkenlights": row of LEDs on a dark chassis, one lit. Nods to mainframe panels.

C — Single glowing LED

Minimalist. One bright green LED on dark. Reads at 16px in the Dock, distinctive, modern.

D — Retro PC front panel

Beige tower face with PWR + HDD LEDs labeled. Most explicit homage, most detail.
